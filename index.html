More than a fishing syndicate

Plan B Angling CIC is a not-for-profit community interest company operating a mixed coarse fishery in Bury St Edmunds.

Any surplus generated by the organisation is reinvested into the fishery, its facilities and fish stocks. Our aim is to make angling available to the community, encourage participation in the sport, and provide an environment where people can benefit physically and mentally from fishing.

The directors are also fishing members. They pay the same fees as everyone else and do not receive payment for running the fishery.

What you can expect

Mixed coarse fishery
About 2 acres

Carp, grass carp, tench, perch, bream, roach, Wels catfish and more.

Membership
Limited numbers

Numbers are deliberately controlled to avoid overcrowding and protect the character of the fishery.

Access
Secure, members only

Secure access from Fornham Road. Pre-arranged visitors must be hosted or escorted.

Interested in joining?

Applications are accepted even when the fishery is at capacity. Suitable applicants can be added to the waiting list and contacted as places become available.
